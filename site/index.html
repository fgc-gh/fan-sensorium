How this is made
  • Heat: surface temperature from Landsat 8 and 9 (USGS Collection 2, band ST_B10), every clear pass from mid June to mid September, through Microsoft Planetary Computer.
  • Cloudy and shadowed pixels are removed using each scene's own quality band. A scene is used only if most of the neighborhood is clear.
  • Each 30 m cell shows the median of those passes, compared with the neighborhood-wide median.
  • Landsat passes over Eugene at about 11:15 AM, so this is late-morning ground temperature, not afternoon air temperature. The afternoon pattern is similar and hotter.
  • The thermal sensor sees about 100 m at a time. Trust differences between blocks, not between next-door lots.
  • Green cover: summer greenness (NDVI) from Sentinel-2 at 10 m, the median of clear passes over the same months. A spot counts as green at NDVI 0.5 or more.
  • Green cover sees anything leafy and growing. In a dry Eugene summer that is mostly trees, but watered lawns and gardens count too. A tree-height layer from lidar, which tells a tree from a lawn, is next.
  • Lot lines: Lane County Assessment & Taxation via LCOG. Neighborhood line: City of Eugene.
